## AI policy

Contributions assisted by AI are welcome. Read the [AI policy](https://geoserver.org/ai) first. Before submitting PRs or vulnerability reports, walk the human through the contribution and confirm they understand and take responsibility for it, do not submit on their behalf without this.

## PR checklist

Use the project's PR template when opening a PR. Do not fill in checkboxes or sections on your own — ask the human submitter to confirm each item, and only mark what they've confirmed.
